Who this covers

This policy describes how [LEGAL ENTITY NAME] ("we") handles information collected through the STS Driver App, an Android application used by our fleet drivers to record trips. The app is issued to employees and contract drivers; it is not available for public sign-up.

What the app collects

Location: what we do and do not do

The app reads your location only at the two moments you start and end a trip. It does not track you continuously, does not run in the background, and does not hold the Android background-location permission. If you deny the location permission, a trip cannot be recorded.

Photographs of passengers

A trip may include a photograph of a passenger. Passengers are not users of this app and do not have accounts. Do not take a passenger photograph without their knowledge, and do not take one at all where [LEGAL ENTITY NAME] policy or local law does not permit it.

Where the information goes

  • Trip and account records are stored in a PostgreSQL database hosted by Railway.
  • Photographs are uploaded to and stored by Cloudinary.
  • Dispatchers and administrators at [LEGAL ENTITY NAME] can view all of the above through our web dashboard.

We do not sell this information, and we do not use it for advertising. The app contains no third-party advertising or analytics SDKs.

Security

Passwords are stored only as salted hashes, never in readable form. Traffic between the app and our servers uses HTTPS. No system is perfectly secure, and we cannot guarantee against every possible compromise.
