Support

Help for drivers using the STS Driver App on iPhone and Android.

Email us for help with the app or your account:

admin@startechsolutions.us

Please include your name, the email you sign in with, and the date and time of the trip if your question is about one.

Getting started

  1. Install the app from the App Store or Google Play.
  2. Sign in with the driver account your dispatcher created for you. The app has no public sign-up.
  3. Allow camera and location access when asked. Both are needed to record a trip.

Common questions

I don't have an account

Accounts are created by StarTech Solutions dispatch. Ask your dispatcher, or email us, and we'll set one up for you.

I forgot my password

On the sign-in screen, tap Forgot your password? and enter your email. We'll send you a link to set a new password. If the email doesn't arrive within a few minutes, check your spam folder, then contact us.

I can't start a trip

Before a trip begins, the app asks for three photos: the license plate, a driver selfie and the starting odometer. The passenger photo is optional. The trip starts once all three required photos are taken. Make sure the app has camera and location access in your phone's settings.

A photo won't upload

Check that you have a mobile or Wi-Fi connection and try again. If it keeps failing, email us with the trip details and roughly when it happened.

My trip or mileage looks wrong

Contact your dispatcher or email us with the trip date. Dispatch can review the trip and its odometer photos.

Accessing or deleting your information

See our Privacy Policy for what the app collects and how long it is kept. To request a copy, a correction or deletion, email us at the address above.
