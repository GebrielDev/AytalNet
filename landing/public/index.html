STS Driver App

Trip logging, odometer capture and GPS for STS fleet drivers.

Get the app on Google Play

The app is in internal testing. Opening this link signs you in to the test and takes you to the Play Store page to install.

Getting started

  1. Install the app on your Android phone.
  2. Sign in with the driver account your dispatcher created for you.
  3. Allow camera and location access — both are required to record a trip.
